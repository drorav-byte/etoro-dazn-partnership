# DAZN working brandbook

## Purpose and status

This is a working campaign brandbook for the DAZN x etoro Germany partnership. It translates the supplied DAZN competition page and publicly available DAZN brand references into practical guidance for the first email and related campaign assets.

It is not an official DAZN corporate brandbook. The DAZN logo files, exact colour values, font files, sport photography rights, lockups, legal wording, and final co-branding rules must be supplied or approved by DAZN before production.

## Brand role in this campaign

DAZN is the hero brand and the reason the user should care. The email must feel like a DAZN sports-fan offer with an etoro qualification step, not like an etoro trading promotion decorated with DAZN colours.

Primary campaign promise:

> Two months of DAZN for qualifying new German etoro clients.

The etoro benefits are supporting proof and retention mechanics. They must appear after the DAZN proposition is understood.

## Visual DNA

### Core impression

- Bold, immediate and high-energy.
- Sports-first, digital-first and built for fans.
- High contrast with strong colour blocking.
- Dynamic rather than corporate or financial.
- Confident and direct, without sounding like a betting or trading ad.

### Colour direction

Use DAZN neon yellow as the lead campaign accent against deep black or near-black. White is the primary text colour on dark surfaces. Use etoro green only as a contained co-brand or CTA accent after the DAZN hero has established the visual frame.

Working palette for mockups only:

| Role | Working value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| DAZN neon yellow | `#F7F500` | Hero accent, offer number, highlight bars, key labels |
| Deep black | `#000000` | Hero field, full-bleed background, strong contrast |
| Soft black | `#101010` | Secondary panels and content blocks |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Main type and negative space |
| etoro green | `#13C636` | Secondary CTA or etoro-owned action, only where approved |

The yellow value is a working approximation, not a supplied DAZN colour token. Do not finalise production artwork from this table without DAZN confirmation.

### Composition

- Prefer full-bleed dark fields and bold yellow interruptions.
- Use large numeric or short headline moments: “2 MONTHS”, “LIVE SPORT”, “YOUR ACCESS”.
- Create visual momentum with diagonals, angled crops, split panels, scorecard-like blocks, and motion-inspired lines.
- Keep one dominant focal point. Avoid a grid of equal-weight benefits above the DAZN offer.
- The co-brand lockup should be compact and secondary to the DAZN hero.
- Use generous negative space around logos and keep all legal text quiet but readable at the bottom.

### Imagery

Use real sports energy: football action, a fan moment, stadium atmosphere, broadcast-style framing, or a close-up that feels captured inside the game. Prefer licensed DAZN or partner-approved imagery.

Avoid:

- Generic finance stock photography.
- Trading screens as the hero image.
- Abstract fintech gradients.
- Lifestyle scenes disconnected from sport.
- Unlicensed football club, competition, player or league marks.
- Imagery that implies betting, gambling or guaranteed financial success.

## Typography and graphic language

DAZN’s public-facing identity is typographically assertive and built for immediate recognition. The exact DAZN typeface must be confirmed by the partner. Until then:

- Use a bold, modern sans serif with strong condensed or display capability for hero headlines.
- Use sentence case or short uppercase display phrases only where the DAZN asset system supports it.
- Keep headlines short and high-impact.
- Use large numbers as visual anchors.
- Keep explanatory copy short, white, and highly legible.
- Never use the etoro brand font as a substitute for a DAZN display font in the DAZN hero.

Recommended hierarchy:

1. DAZN offer headline.
2. Two-month value and qualification condition.
3. Primary CTA.
4. Short etoro qualification explanation.
5. Supporting benefits and retention detail.
6. Legal and risk copy.

## Motion and energy

DAZN’s brand expression is associated with movement, live sport and the chase of the game. In static email, express this through composition rather than decorative effects:

- Angled yellow bars or cropped blocks.
- A directional subject crop.
- Scoreboard-like numeric emphasis.
- A strong diagonal entry into the hero.
- Short, active verbs.

Do not use neon glows, 3D gaming effects, flames, casino cues, or cluttered trading charts.

## Voice and copy

### DAZN-first voice

- Direct.
- Energetic.
- Fan-aware.
- Clear about access and timing.
- Focused on the experience of watching sport.

Example direction:

> Your next match starts here.

> Get two months of DAZN with etoro.

> Live sport. One exclusive partnership benefit.

The financial mechanics should be introduced in a quieter secondary section. Do not lead with “deposit”, “bonus”, “commission”, “ETF”, or “returns”.

### Words and ideas to avoid

- Guaranteed returns.
- Risk-free.
- Nothing to lose.
- Win money.
- Bet, jackpot, odds, stake, or gamble.
- Trading platform as the main experience.
- Crypto or CFD in this campaign.

## Co-branding with etoro

The relationship should read as:

> DAZN presents an exclusive benefit available through etoro.

Not:

> etoro sells a financial promotion using DAZN as decoration.

Recommended email structure:

1. Compact DAZN x etoro partnership lockup.
2. DAZN-led hero with “2 months free DAZN”.
3. CTA to qualify or get started.
4. Simple qualification explanation.
5. Supporting etoro benefits: €20 airdrop, 50% commission discount, zero-commission ETF, and six-month bonus.
6. Football-led retention explanation.
7. Full terms and risk information.

The DAZN logo must not be redrawn, recoloured, distorted, placed on a busy background, or combined with the etoro logo without approved clear space and lockup rules.

## First-email design direction

### Hero

- Near-black full-width background.
- DAZN yellow as the most visible accent.
- Large football or live-sport visual, preferably partner-approved.
- Headline: “2 months of DAZN” or approved German equivalent.
- Supporting line: “Exclusive through the DAZN x etoro partnership.”
- CTA: short, direct, and visibly secondary to the offer message.

### Supporting section

Present etoro as the way to keep the journey going after the user claims DAZN:

- Choose from the €20 airdrop selection after signup.
- Build a monthly investment habit with eligible ETFs.
- Track the six-month qualifying period.

Keep the supporting cards visually lighter than the hero. The first screen should not look like a product comparison table or trading dashboard.

### Deadline

Use the 30 September deadline as a clear yellow timing marker, not as a panic or scarcity device:

> Arrive by 30 September to access the exclusive partnership benefits.

## Accessibility and email requirements

- Preserve strong yellow-on-black and white-on-black contrast.
- Do not place essential copy inside an image only.
- Provide alt text for sports imagery and logos.
- Make the two-month DAZN offer legible without scrolling on mobile.
- Test desktop, mobile and narrow-mobile layouts.
- Keep legal and risk text readable and separate from decorative sport graphics.

## Approval gates

Before release, obtain:

- Official DAZN logo and co-brand lockup guidance.
- Approved DAZN colour and typography tokens.
- Rights-cleared hero imagery or approved DAZN assets.
- German DAZN offer wording and redemption terms.
- Approval for use of competition, football, league, team and player references.
- Approval of etoro offer terms, bonus mechanics and financial disclaimers.
