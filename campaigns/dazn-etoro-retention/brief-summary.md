# DAZN x etoro Germany Retention

## Campaign proposition

DAZN gives sport fans a reason to join. etoro gives them a reason to stay.

The first communication is DAZN-first: two months of DAZN in Germany. The €20 first-deposit reward and six-month challenge are supporting benefits and should not compete with the DAZN headline.

## Reward architecture

- Qualifying new German users can receive two months of DAZN.
- A qualifying first deposit can trigger a €20 reward, subject to approved terms.
- The proposed six-month challenge asks users to make a qualifying deposit/investment each month for six months, with a 10% promotional reward on the qualifying amount, capped at €500.
- Germany-facing materials should use euros. The final qualifying base is unresolved.

## Audience and product progression

The audience is sport-led, mobile-first, and likely mixed in investing confidence. Product education should progress from simple, long-term investing in eligible stocks and ETFs, to recurring investments, diversified portfolios, CopyTrader, and—only if confirmed eligible and approved—fixed-income options.

Sport is the narrative thread: build a portfolio like a team, invest one matchday at a time, diversify beyond one player, and stay in the game for six months. These are metaphors, not promises of performance.

## Journey and channels

1. Acquisition email: DAZN hero, €20 reward secondary.
2. Activation email and in-app card: complete registration, deposit, and DAZN redemption.
3. First-investment email: explain stocks, ETFs, risk, and how to start.
4. Monthly email/push: challenge progress and next qualifying action.
5. Product education: recurring investments first; CopyTrader and other products later based on behaviour.
6. DAZN expiry sequence: final-month reminders and transition to ongoing etoro value.
7. Post-challenge email: continue the investing habit beyond the promotion.

## Compliance boundary

Do not say “guaranteed 10% returns” or imply that an investment will grow by 10%. The proposed benefit is a promotional reward/cashback tied to qualifying activity; underlying investments can rise or fall and capital is at risk. Legal, regulatory, product, German-language, tax, and partner approval are required before launch.
