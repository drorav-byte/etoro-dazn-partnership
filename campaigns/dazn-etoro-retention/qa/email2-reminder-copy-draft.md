The opening is a short reminder for the unused DAZN coupon; the main section then shifts to the etoro products introduced in Email 1 and explains them briefly for people who are new to investing.

- Preheader: `Your DAZN code is still waiting. Your €20 reward is already applied. See what's next.`

## Copy hierarchy

1. Establish the progression: the €20 reward is already applied, and the DAZN subscription is the first benefit still waiting to be used.
2. Keep the DAZN reminder as a short paragraph with the dynamic code inline and a `Use it on DAZN` hyperlink rather than a large coupon card.
