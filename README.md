# DAZN x etoro Germany campaign

Planning repository for the DAZN-led acquisition and retention campaign targeting the first batch of 7,000 sport fans in Germany.

## Campaign proposition

DAZN gives users a reason to join. etoro gives them a reason to stay.

The campaign leads with the two-month DAZN benefit. The €20 first-deposit reward and six-month investing challenge support activation and retention but should not compete with DAZN as the primary message.

All communications should use sport-related framing, including when explaining investing, recurring investments, diversification, CopyTrader, and portfolio construction.

## Review focus

Please add comments or open issues for:

1. The exact DAZN plan, eligibility, redemption flow, duration, and partner terms.
2. The €20 first-deposit reward type, qualifying rules, timing, reversals, and budget.
3. Whether the six-month 10% promotional reward is calculated on deposits, invested amounts, or both.
4. The reward cap, monthly qualification, completion rules, and German legal/compliance treatment.
5. German product eligibility and approved positioning for stocks, ETFs, recurring investments, CopyTrader, and fixed-income products.
6. Audience selection, control-group design, event tracking, and retention KPIs.

## Important compliance boundary

The 10% benefit must not be presented as guaranteed investment performance, a risk-free return, or a promise that an investment will grow. Working language is **promotional reward** or **cashback on qualifying activity**, subject to approved terms. Underlying investments can rise or fall and capital is at risk.
