# DAZN x etoro Germany Campaign Skill

This skill covers the DAZN-led acquisition and six-month retention campaign targeting 7,000 German sport fans.

## Campaign Proposition

**DAZN gives sport fans a reason to join. etoro gives them a reason to stay.**

The first communication is always DAZN-first. The two-month DAZN benefit is the headline. The €20 first-deposit reward and six-month investing challenge are supporting benefits and must not compete with the DAZN message.

---

## Message Hierarchy

| Priority | Message |
|---|---|
| 1 | Two months of DAZN (primary acquisition hook) |
| 2 | €20 first-deposit reward (activation support) |
| 3 | Six-month investing challenge with promotional reward (retention) |

---

## Reward Architecture

- **DAZN benefit**: Two months of DAZN for qualifying new German users.
- **First-deposit reward**: €20 reward on a qualifying first deposit, subject to approved terms.
- **Six-month challenge**: Qualifying deposit/investment each month for six months → 10% promotional reward on qualifying amount, capped at €500.
- All Germany-facing materials use euros.
- The qualifying base (deposit amount vs invested amount) is pending confirmation.

> ⚠️ **Compliance boundary**: Never say "guaranteed 10% returns" or imply an investment will grow by 10%. Describe the benefit as a **promotional reward** or **cashback on qualifying activity**. Underlying investments can rise or fall; capital is at risk.

---

## Target Audience

- 7,000 German sport fans (first batch)
- Mixed investing confidence — many may be new to investing
- Mobile-first, sport-led
- Not necessarily from a trading background

---

## Product Progression

Educate in this order — do not skip ahead:

1. Eligible stocks and ETFs (long-term, simple)
2. Recurring investments
3. Diversified portfolios
4. CopyTrader
5. Fixed-income options (only if confirmed eligible and approved for Germany)

---

## Sport-Led Copy Direction

Sport is the narrative thread across all communications. Use these as guiding metaphors:

- "Build your portfolio like a team."
- "Invest one matchday at a time."
- "Don't rely on one player — diversify."
- "See how other investors build their line-up."
- "Your DAZN offer is nearing the final whistle. Your investing journey can continue."
- "Stay in the game for six months."

These are metaphors, not promises of performance.

## Open Issues (confirm before launch)

1. Exact DAZN plan, eligibility, redemption flow, duration, and partner terms.
2. Whether the €20 reward is an airdrop, cash credit, or another reward type.
3. German legal/compliance approval for the 10% mechanic — must be described as promotional cashback, not guaranteed investment performance.
4. Qualifying base: deposit amount, invested amount, or both; cap, timing, reversals, and tax treatment.
5. German availability and approved positioning for all promoted assets.
